01 / A REALITY FOR MILLIONS

About 1 in 7people live withmigraine

More than a headache. Migraine is a neurological condition that can bring throbbing pain, nausea, and sensitivity to light or sound.

Learn more · The Migraine Trust ↗
02 / WHEN SIGNALS FEEL LOUDER

A sensitive
pain pathway.

Migraine involves changes in nerve signaling. The trigeminal nerve helps carry pain signals from structures around the brain and face.

Migraine is a complex neurological condition—not a blockage or a single spot where pain lives.

Explore migraine · NINDS ↗
03 / SIMILAR FEELING. DIFFERENT CAUSE.

Pressure around
your sinuses.

Sinuses are air-filled spaces around the nose. When their lining becomes inflamed, congestion and facial pressure can follow.

Sinusitis and migraine are different conditions. Migraine can also feel like sinus pressure.

Understand sinusitis · MedlinePlus ↗
04 / A LITTLE COMFORT, CLOSE TO YOU

Cool contact.
A quieter moment.

A chilled cap absorbs heat at the skin’s surface. Cold packs may help ease migraine pain; the cap’s soft coverage also shields your eyes from light.

BeyondMeds offers comfort, not a cure for migraine or sinusitis. It does not clear sinus congestion. Prepare and use it as directed.
